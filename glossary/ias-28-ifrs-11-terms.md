# Glossary / المصطلحات

| Term | العربية | Meaning (own wording) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Associate | شركة زميلة | An entity over which the investor has significant influence but not control. | ST p.385 |
| Significant influence | تأثير جوهري | Power to take part in the investee's financial and operating policy decisions, without control or joint control. 20% or more of the votes is presumed to give it. | ST p.385 |
| Equity method | طريقة حقوق الملكية | Investment shown at cost plus the investor's share of the investee's post-acquisition change in net assets. | Kit fp.170 |
| Joint control | سيطرة مشتركة | Contractually agreed sharing of control; decisions on the relevant activities need unanimous consent. | Kit fp.256 |
| Joint operation | عملية مشتركة | Parties have direct rights to assets and obligations for liabilities; each recognises its share of the lines. | Kit fp.256-257 |
| Joint venture | مشروع مشترك | Parties have rights to the net assets; each uses the equity method. | Kit fp.256-257 |
| Unrealised profit | ربح غير محقق | Profit on trading with the associate not yet earned outside the group; eliminated only to the investor's share. | Kit fp.187 |
