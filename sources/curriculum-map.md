**Page reference convention used in this project**
- **ST p.X** = printed page number of the Study Text. The file's `<!-- page N -->` marker = printed page + 20 (checked at every chapter start, e.g. Ch 3 Revenue: printed 39 = file marker 59).
- **Kit fp.X** = the Kit file's `<!-- page N -->` marker (printed page numbers in the Kit are inconsistent after OCR, so file markers are used).
- Both files are OCR conversions of PDFs. Some characters are corrupted (e.g. "I" for "1", "Bl O" for "B10", "ß" in words). Paragraph references inside the text (e.g. "IFRS 18: para. 47") can be cited, but each must be checked against the text when used.

## 5. Conflicts, gaps and warnings / التعارضات والملاحظات

| # | Issue | Evidence | Effect on the website |

So this is not a truncated upload. The text still refers readers to "Chapter 23" (ST p.386) and "IFRS 11: Chapter 24", and the exam-analysis grid lists those topics, but no such chapters exist in this copy. ## 6. Proposed priority order for the standard pages / ترتيب الأولوية المقترح

Based on: (a) relevance to an external auditor in Egypt (as requested), (b) exam weight (Q1 is always consolidation = 25%), (c) number of Kit questions.

**Wave 1 — core (auditor + exam)**
1. IFRS 15 Revenue (Ch 3; 8 Kit/mock questions)
2. IFRS 9 + IAS 32 + IFRS 13 Financial instruments & fair value (Ch 10; 11+ questions)
3. IFRS 16 Leases (Ch 6; 6 questions)
4. IFRS 3 + IFRS 10 Business combinations and consolidation (Ch 7 §3, Ch 20–22; Q1 of every exam, 14+ questions)
5. IAS 12 Income taxes (Ch 11; 5 questions)
6. IAS 36 Impairment (Ch 5; 13 questions incl. consolidation)
7. IAS 37 Provisions (Ch 8; 8 questions)

**Wave 2 — high exam frequency**
8. IFRS 18 Presentation (new; effective 1 Jan 2027)
9. IAS 16 / IAS 23 / IAS 20 / IAS 40 (Ch 4)
10. IAS 38 Intangibles (Ch 7)
11. IAS 19 Employee benefits (Ch 9)
12. IAS 8 / IFRS 5 / IAS 10 (Ch 16)
13. IFRS 2 Share-based payment (Ch 14)
14. IAS 21 Foreign currency (Ch 12)
15. IAS 33 EPS (Ch 17)

**Wave 3 — lower weight**
16. IAS 2, IAS 41, IFRS 6 (Ch 13)
17. IAS 24, IFRS 8 (Ch 18)
18. IFRS for SMEs, IFRS 19 (Ch 19, Ch 20 §2.10)
19. Conceptual Framework, IASB/ISSB (IFRS S1/S2), Ethics (Ch 1–2)

**Partly covered:** IAS 28 and IFRS 11 have a Kit-only page at `ifrs-study/ias/ias-28-ifrs-11-from-kit.md`. **Still limited because this edition has no chapters on them:** full IAS 28/IFRS 11 detail, IAS 27, IFRS 12 (C1).
